Friends of Caycuma

For the small angels with no home — yet

Caycuma is a small town with a big heart and many street cats and dogs. Friends of Caycuma is the practical arm of Healing Angels: food, foster, and forever homes.

Donate

A bowl of food, a warm night

Every gift — large or small — buys food, medicine and shelter for a cat or dog in Caycuma. Cats and dogs are both cared for here; no one is left out.

Donations are handled securely. You can give once or set up a small monthly gift that quietly keeps a rescue fed.

Give €10 Give €25 Give €50 Custom

All donations go directly to cat & dog food, vet care, and warm bedding in Caycuma.

How your gift helps

  • €10 — a week of kibble for one street cat
  • €25 — a vet check + flea treatment for a dog
  • €50 — neuter a cat or dog, helping the whole colony
  • €100 — emergency surgery contribution
Volunteer

Give your time

Even one hour helps. Feed, walk, foster, transport, or simply sit with a shy rescue until it trusts again.

The owner will receive your offer and reply to you by email.
